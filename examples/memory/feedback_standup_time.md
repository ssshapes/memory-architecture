Standup starts at 9:15.

**Why:** the west-coast half of the team is asleep at 9:00, so a 9:00 standup is
a meeting half the people attend badly. Tried it for a month; it never once
worked.

**How to apply:** when scheduling anything recurring that both coasts attend,
start no earlier than 9:15. If something has to be at 9:00, it is a one-coast
meeting and should be named as one.
